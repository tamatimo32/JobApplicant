Reports to: Global Procurement Manager
Business area: Electrification – Distribution Solutions (ELDS) global procurement
Posted 2026-11-05 | Category: Sustainability

YOUR ROLE AND RESPONSIBILITIES
Manage and coordinate sustainability initiatives across the global procurement
organisation of Electrification – Distribution Solutions (ELDS). Focus on
supplier sustainability performance, responsible sourcing and reducing
emissions. Translate ABB sustainability objectives into procurement programmes
and supplier actions. Act as the sustainability advisor for ELDS Procurement
and support implementation, reporting, compliance and continuous improvement
across the division, regions and supplier base.

Main stakeholders: global and regional procurement teams, Category Managers,
suppliers, internal sustainability and other functions, external bodies and
authorities.

Accountable for:
- Defining and advancing the ELDS global procurement sustainability roadmap,
  aligned with ABB sustainability objectives.
- Integrating sustainability requirements into procurement processes, sourcing
  strategies, supplier management, category plans and contracts; partnering
  with Category Managers to embed ESG criteria and responsible sourcing.
- Managing supplier sustainability assessments, risk evaluations, corrective
  action plans and relevant programmes, including Sustainable Supply Base
  Management (SSBM) and material compliance.
- Setting and deploying sustainability objectives and KPIs across ELDS
  Procurement; regular follow-up and tracking of supplier performance,
  including EcoVadis coverage where applicable.
- Developing methodologies, baselines, targets and improvement roadmaps for
  supplier-related emissions, with a focus on ELDS Scope 3 Category 1 reduction.
- Ensuring reliable sustainability data collection and producing regular
  performance reports and management dashboards.
- Monitoring emerging regulations, reporting requirements and customer
  expectations; coordinating procurement readiness and advising on
  sustainability risks, obligations and mitigation actions.
- Engaging internal and external stakeholders, representing Procurement in
  relevant forums, and providing guidance, training and coaching to support
  behavioural change and continuous improvement.

QUALIFICATIONS
- Experience in procurement sustainability, responsible sourcing, or
  sustainability management in an international supply-chain environment.
- Knowledge of supplier ESG performance, sustainability assessments, risk
  evaluation and corrective action planning; experience with SSBM and material
  compliance.
- Understanding of supplier-related emissions measurement and reduction,
  particularly Scope 3 Category 1 (Purchased Goods and Services).
- Comfortable with sustainability KPIs and data: collection, analysis,
  performance tracking, reporting or dashboard development.
- Can interpret sustainability regulations, reporting requirements and customer
  expectations and translate them into procurement actions.
- Communicates effectively, builds commitment with internal and external
  stakeholders, can guide, train and coach teams to support behavioural change.
- Right attitude, initiative and motivation.
- Fluent written and spoken English; proficient in MS365, particularly Excel,
  PowerPoint and Power BI.
