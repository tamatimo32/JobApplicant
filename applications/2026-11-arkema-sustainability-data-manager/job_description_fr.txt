/ Sécurité. Publié le 7 nov. 2026.

Le poste est rattaché à Adhesive Solutions (Bostik). L'annonce ne demande pas
de lettre de motivation ; candidature via jobs.arkema.com.

DÉTAILS DE LA MISSION
Support sustainability data management and product portfolio assessments for
Adhesive Solutions on an 18-month fixed-term contract, ensuring reliable data
for regulatory, environmental, and business needs. Regular interaction with
multiple internal functions to collect, validate, and consolidate
sustainability data. The role also improves efficiency, scalability and
reliability by identifying process simplification, standardization and
automation opportunities.

Customer support (25 %)
- Prepare data and documentation for customer CSR requests, respond to
  customer online questionnaires with Corporate Sustainable Development and
  BU CSR teams
- Optimize generation, availability and retention of standardized LCA documents
- Track activity on sustainability data requests (workflows, document access,
  follow-up)
- Contribute to standardizing and automating recurring customer requests

Portfolio Sustainability Assessment (PSA) (50 %)
- Support regulatory evaluations with PSRA and BU CSR leaders
- Record and maintain PSA in the Archimedes tool, including quarterly sales
  data updates within segmentations
- Flag regulatory risks and improvement opportunities in product portfolios
- Perform assessments and simulations for new R&D products
- Improve and automate data exploitation and maintenance

Sustainability data excellence & automation (25 %)
- Improve data quality, tools, processes and automation
- Contribute to internal reporting, dashboards and KPI monitoring
- Support supplier information data management: identify and follow up
  missing sustainability documents with PSRA and Procurement
- Support ad hoc analyses
- Implement digital solutions, standardize processes, improve accessibility
  and traceability

PROFIL RECHERCHÉ
- Master en sciences de l'environnement, génie chimique, chimie, science des
  matériaux ou domaine technique connexe
- Première expérience professionnelle ou solide bagage académique en Product
  Stewardship et/ou méthodologies PCF (Product Carbon Footprint)
- Gérer, analyser et restituer de gros volumes de données, plusieurs priorités
  en parallèle
- Bon relationnel, efficacité en organisation matricielle
- Communication écrite et orale claire

FIT NOTES (pour le relecteur, pas pour la lettre)
Bon recouvrement sur le PCF : ISO 14067, GHG Protocol, outil Power BI
d'empreinte carbone produit, automatisation, données fournisseurs, reporting
et KPI. Écarts : pas d'ACV complète, pas d'Archimedes, pas de Product
Stewardship au sens strict, pas de chimie. CDD 18 mois à Puteaux.
