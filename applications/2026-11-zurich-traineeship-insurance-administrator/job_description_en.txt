ABOUT THE ROLE
Underwriting Services (UWS), Zaventem. The department handles all
administration after an insurance deal closes. Over about one year the trainee
does three rotations across three sub-teams (Domestic, International and
European team).

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Collecting data and drawing up policies and endorsements
- Processing financial data and issuing invoices
- Working with other departments and serving customers and insurance brokers
- Coordinating international insurance programmes for large Belgian multinationals
- Looking continuously at how processes can be improved, presenting ideas to the team

ABOUT YOU
- Dutch and/or French, plus excellent English (reading and writing)
- Recently obtained a Bachelor or Master degree or equivalent (graduaat)
- Maximum 2 years of work experience; insurance knowledge is a plus, not required
- Analytically strong, detail oriented, comfortable with numbers
- Service-oriented, proactive, can-do mentality

FIT NOTES (for the reviewer, not for the letter)
The CV is environmental and sustainability focused. There is no insurance
experience and no Dutch. The posting allows no insurance knowledge and asks for
